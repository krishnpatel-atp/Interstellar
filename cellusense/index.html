      How CelluSense works

      The bandage

      A carboxymethyl-cellulose hydrogel infused with red-cabbage anthocyanin and turmeric curcumin, cross-linked with citric acid. It stays rosy-purple in acidic, healthy wounds and shifts toward blue/indigo/green as pH rises — an early sign of bacterial infection.

      The algorithm

      The printed white and black rings have known colours. Comparing what the camera sees to those known values gives a per-channel correction (in linear light) that removes colour casts, brightness changes and shadows. The corrected pad colour gives Index = Rcenter / Gcenter, which the calibration curve converts to pH.

      Risk zones

      Green pH 5.5–6.2 normal healing · Yellow pH 6.3–7.2 flag for observation · Red pH ≥ 7.3 notify care team.

      CelluSense is a student research prototype and is not a certified medical device.
